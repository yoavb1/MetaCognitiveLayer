Informed Consent Agreement

Research Study: Human-AI Collaboration in Process Anomaly Detection

Purpose of the Study: You are invited to participate in a research evaluation studying supervisory decision-making in industrial telemetry environments. The session involves monitoring live data streams and taking system interventions.

Procedures: You will complete a series of short simulation trials where you will monitor real-time industrial sensors, evaluate AI recommendations, and determine if an emergency override or system stop is required.

Data & Privacy: All logged reaction times, control actions, and interaction timings are completely anonymized. No personally identifiable details are permanently bound to telemetry outputs.

Voluntary Participation: Participation is strictly voluntary. You may end the session at any time without penalty by closing your browser.
