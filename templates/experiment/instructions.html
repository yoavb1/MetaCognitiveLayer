System Stack & Telemetry Flow

Detailed breakdown of the monitoring hardware and operating framework

10-Sensor Integrated Array

The physical installation monitored by your console consists of 10 distinct sensors measuring critical operational parameters (temperature, flow rate, pressure, vibration, etc.).

Sampling Rate / Update Frequency: Every 2.0 Seconds
The Operational Management System

Running behind the dashboard is an Operational Intelligence System. It constantly sweeps incoming data streams from all 10 sensors to calculate risk metrics and identify system anomalies before catastrophic damage occurs.

  • Evaluates baseline standard deviations across sensor groups.
  • Calculates instantaneous process risk scores.
  • Triggers visual and auditory alarms upon identifying abnormal behavior.
